Questions we get a lot

Quick answers to the things farmers usually ring about. Anything else, call Andrew.

When should I book my first cut?

Early. First-cut slots go fast once the season warms up, so ring as soon as you have a rough idea of timing. Mid-season top-ups are usually easier to fit in.

What areas do you cover?

Paddock work runs across Mid Canterbury from our base in Tinwald. Transport goes further, we cart South Island wide.

Can you take maize from planting through to the stack?

Yes. We plant it, chop it, cart it and stack it. Booking planting and the silage contract together in one call is the easiest way to do it.

My block is small or hard to get at. Is that a problem?

Usually not. We mow remote and awkward country, so describe the block before you rule it out. Worst case we tell you straight that it will not work.

How do I get a price?

Ring Andrew with your rough hectares and timing, or use the email button below with the same details. You get a straight answer, not a sales pitch.
